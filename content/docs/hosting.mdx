tourney runs an osu! tournament in one place: registration, teams, qualifiers, the bracket, the schedule and results.

## Start

Sign in with osu!, then pick **Host a tournament**. Make a lineage (the series, like "Evergreen Cup") and its first edition (one year of it). Each account can run one edition at a time; ask on the Discord server to raise that.

## Setup

A new edition starts in setup. Its checklist shows what's missing before registration can open.
