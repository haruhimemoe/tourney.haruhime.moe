How to play in a tournament hosted on tourney.

## Find a tournament

[Browse](/browse) lists every tournament taking registrations now, with its mode, team size, rank range and when registration closes.

## Register

Open the tournament's page and pick **Register**. You sign in with your osu! account through haruhime.moe. The form shows the host's questions and who may register (rank, country or region). If you're outside those limits, it says which one.

For a team tournament, the captain registers the whole team: its name, an optional tag, and each member's osu! ID.

When you register, tourney saves a snapshot of your osu! username, rank and country. The host sees it as it was that day, even if your rank changes later.

You can change your answers or withdraw while registration is open.

## Set when you're free

Your home page has an availability grid for each tournament you registered for: 7 days by 24 hours in your time zone. Click or drag to mark the hours you're free, then save. Hosts use it to suggest match times that suit both teams.

## Find your match

Your home page shows, for each tournament:

- whether you're approved, waitlisted or still waiting,
- your team,
- your next match, its opponent and its time in your time zone,
- the round's pool on pools.haruhime.moe, once the host reveals it.

The tournament's own page has the bracket, the full schedule, every team, the pools and the results. Each match has its own page with its time, score, picks and bans, the maps played, and the mp, stream and VOD links.
