### How to use them

Exporting or deleting your haruhime account on [haruhime.moe/account](https://www.haruhime.moe/account) covers your tourney data too, as [Privacy](/legal/privacy) describes.

Your name and avatar come from your osu! profile. Change them on osu!, and haruhime.moe copies the new ones the next time you sign in.

tourney is small and free, and may fall below the thresholds where the CCPA applies. We give these rights to everyone anyway, wherever you live.
