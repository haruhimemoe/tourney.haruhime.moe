## Moderation

Admins can hide or delete any lineage or edition that breaks these rules, and remove an account that keeps breaking them.

## Deleting

Deleting your haruhime account on haruhime.moe/account deletes your tourney data, as [Privacy](/legal/privacy) describes. Deleting happens at once and can't be undone.
