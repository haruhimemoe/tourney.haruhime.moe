## Tournaments

Hosts run their own tournaments here. Their rules, schedules and results are theirs, not ours. Ask the host about a ruling.

## Ranks and countries

A registration's rank and country are a snapshot from the osu! API taken when the player registered, never refreshed.

## How our requests identify themselves

Our server sends `tourney.haruhime.moe (+https://tourney.haruhime.moe; haruhime@haruhime.moe)` as its User-Agent on every request it makes: to the osu! API (to look up a player when they register, and to read a multiplayer match when a host fills results from an mp link, with tourney's own osu! app), to pools.haruhime.moe (to read the mappools a tournament links), and to haruhime.moe (to end your session there when you sign out on tourney).
